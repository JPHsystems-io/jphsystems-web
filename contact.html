Contact

Tell me what’s exposed.

A couple of paragraphs are enough: what you run, what changed recently, and what keeps you up. I answer every message myself, usually within one business day.

Start a conversation

This form composes an email in your own mail app and sends it to my inbox directly. Nothing is posted to this site and no third party sees it on the way: no CRM, no newsletter, no analytics. If you would rather write it yourself, the address is in the panel beside the form.

Write directly

This form only builds the email — your own mail app sends it. No relay, no third-party processor, no tracking pixels, no newsletter sign-up.

  • Email jphermans@jphsystems.io
  • Response time Usually within one business day. If you are in an active incident, put “incident” in the subject and I will prioritise triage.
  • Availability Two engagements per quarter, plus a small number of advisory slots.
  • Working style Remote-first, with recorded walkthroughs and shared screens. On-site by arrangement for physical network changes.
  • Before you send No credentials, private keys, customer records or production data in a first message. There will be a secure channel once we have a scope.

What to include

  • Roughly how many people and sites you support
  • What the estate runs on: cloud, office, both
  • The thing that changed recently, if anything
  • Any deadline you are working towards
  • Whether you have an internal IT team or an MSP

No Pressure

You do not need a full brief to start.

A sentence about the system and a sentence about the worry is enough for a first reply. We can fill in the rest together.
