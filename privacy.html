Privacy

What this site stores, and what it does not.

Short version: no analytics, no tracking cookies, no third-party requests and no form relay. An enquiry is composed in your own mail client and arrives in an inbox.

Who is responsible

JPHsystems is the trading name of Jean-Pierre Hermans, an independent practice based in Belgium. For anything on this page, including a request to delete something, write to jphermans@jphsystems.io.

The enquiry form

The form on the contact page sends nothing to a server. Pressing the button builds an email from the fields in your own mail client, with the address, the subject line and the body already filled in. Your message travels from that client to my inbox directly. There is no form relay in the middle, so no third party processes your enquiry and no copy of it sits on this site.

Your text stays in the form on screen until you close the page. If your mail client does not open, the page offers the draft link again and the plain address, so nothing you typed is lost.

What is stored in your browser

One thing: the light or dark theme you chose, in localStorage under the key theme. It is read before the page paints so the site does not flash the wrong colours. It holds the word light or dark, nothing else, it is never sent anywhere, and clearing site data removes it.

This site sets no cookies. There is no analytics script, no tag manager, no advertising pixel and no session recording.

Fonts, images and other requests

Fonts, styles, scripts and images are served from this domain. No Google Fonts, no CDN and no embedded video, so visiting a page does not tell a third party who you are. The only outbound links are to the App Store and GitHub, and those policies apply once you click them.

Server logs

The files are published on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Both keep standard access logs — IP address, user agent, requested path, timestamp — to deliver the site and to defend it against abuse, under their own privacy terms. I read them only when something is being hammered, and there is no analytics product attached that would build a profile of visitors.

Emails you send

An enquiry sits in my mailbox, and I use it for one thing: answering it. If we work together, the engagement agreement says what is kept for how long and what gets deleted at handover. If we do not, ask and I will delete the thread.

Apps

Data handled by the apps themselves is outside this notice. Each app has its own statement, and the App Store listing is the place to read it before you install.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of what I hold about you, have it corrected or erased, object to how it is used, or ask for it in a portable format. Write to the address above and I will answer within a month. If you think the answer is wrong, you can complain to the Belgian data protection authority, gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be.

Changes

If the site starts doing something new — an embedded video, an analytics service, a newsletter — this page changes in the same commit as the code, and the date below changes with it.
