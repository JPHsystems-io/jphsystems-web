Token Ledger

OpenRouter usage tracker for iPhone and iPad.

  • Platforms. iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo, built natively in SwiftUI. iOS 27.0 or later.
  • Price. Free. No in-app purchases, no subscription.
  • Category. Developer Tools on the App Store.
  • Who makes it. JPHsystems — designed, built, shipped and supported by the same engineer you hire.

What it does

Everything the API doesn’t tell you.

openrouter.ai gives you a bill. Token Ledger shows you where the money went, which tool spent it, and what each call really cost.

Balance & spending

Remaining credit or key limit, lifetime and monthly spend, and a low-balance banner that turns amber below $10 and red below $5.

Usage charts

Daily spend and token bars with a period average and a trend line. Tap a day to see the prompt, completion and reasoning split.

Account log

OpenRouter bills per API key, so give every tool its own key and the log becomes a per-app view: today, 7-day, month and lifetime spend, plus key limits and expiry.

Model catalogue

All 400+ models with real per-million-token prices, capability filters for vision, reasoning, developer and free models, search and sorting.

Two providers

OpenRouter and TrustedRouter, or both: remaining credit, prepaid versus BYOK split, per-key limits and a switcher that keeps every account separate.

Privacy first

Keys live in the iOS keychain on the device. No account, no analytics, no third-party SDK, and nothing about your usage reaches me.

Built for the foldable

Ready for a screen that changes shape.

Token Ledger is built against the iOS 27 SDK, so iPhone Duo is not an afterthought: the app uses the whole display, and nothing is sized to a named device.

  • Both displays, one app. It fills the inner display and returns to the outer one with your place kept.
  • Two columns when there is room. Balance and charts pair with the account log on a wide window; anything narrower stays single column.
  • Nothing under the hinge. On a partly folded Duo the content is pushed clear of the fold instead of being laid across it.
  • Bars handled. Refresh and Done stay reachable when the navigation bars move down the side.
  • Also works in iPad split view, Stage Manager and iPhone Mirroring.
